THE WAY WE PLAY

A little domino etiquette.

  1. You and Ally play as a team, seated across from each other.
  2. Everyone gets seven tiles. The double-six opens the first round. Turns move counter-clockwise.
  3. Select a highlighted tile, then choose an end on the table or below your hand. When both ends match, either is available. No legal move? Pass your turn.
  4. Empty your hand to win the round. Your team scores the pips in everyone else's hand.
  5. Four consecutive passes block the round. The lowest hand wins; ties favor the team that last played. All remaining pips count.
  6. The first team to the target score wins. You can also play a single round.

Controls: click a tile in your hand or on the 3D table. Keys 1-7 select a tile; Enter plays when only one end is available. With a mouse, drag a hand tile to a highlighted end and release to play. Hover or focus an end to preview the placement. Drag the patio to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. Use Table view for an overhead view.
