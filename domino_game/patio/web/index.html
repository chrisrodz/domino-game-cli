THE WAY WE PLAY

A little domino etiquette.

  1. You and Ally play as a team, seated across from each other.
  2. Everyone gets seven tiles. In the first round, whoever holds the double-six opens with it. After that, the winner of the previous round opens with any tile. Turns move counter-clockwise.
  3. Match a tile to either open end. When both ends match, either is yours. No legal tile? Pass.
  4. Empty your hand to win the round: your team scores every pip still held at the table.
  5. When no one can play, the round is trancado. The team holding fewer pips wins all the pips on the table, and its player with fewer pips opens next. If the teams tie, the team that closed the game wins.
  6. The first team to the target score wins. Doscientos is 200. You can also play a single round.

Controls: click a highlighted tile at your edge of the table, then click a glowing end. Clicking the selected tile again plays it when only one end fits. Keys: 1-7 select, arrows step through playable tiles, L or R plays an end, Enter plays when only one end fits, P passes. Press ? for every shortcut. Drag the patio to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.
